## 4. Direct vs. Third-Party Cost Demarcation

- **Ayantra Development Fee:** Covers all design, coding, asset structuring, and initial deployment implementation.
- **Domain Name Registration:** Direct client expense paid annually via the domain registrar (e.g., GoDaddy, Namecheap).
- **Hosting & Cloud Infrastructure:** Direct client expense billed monthly or annually by the cloud provider (e.g., Vercel, AWS, Hostinger).
- **Third-Party Service Subscriptions:** API credits, email services, SMS gateways, and CRM licenses are paid directly to the respective providers.
- **Post-Launch Maintenance (Optional):** Retainer-based maintenance, regular updates, and bug fixes available at ₹2,000 per month.

## 5. Revision Policy & Scope Definitions

- **Definition of a Revision:** Modifications to existing copy, visual layouts, colors, fonts, or component placements within the agreed scope.
- **Definition of a New Requirement:** Additions of new pages, features, workflows, or integrations not specified in the original agreement.
- **Out-of-Scope Billing:** Additional revision cycles or scope additions are billed at an hourly rate of ₹1,000 per hour or quoted as a separate add-on module.
